### 三、计算题

**1.** 
（1）由牛顿第二定律 $m\dfrac{dv}{dt} = -kv$，得 $v(t) = v_0 e^{-kt/m} = 10e^{-2t}\,\text{m/s}$

（2）$x = \int_0^\infty 10e^{-2t}\,dt = \dfrac{v_0 m}{k} = \dfrac{10 \times 2}{4} = 5\,\text{m}$

---

**2.**
（1）$\omega = \sqrt{k/m} = \sqrt{200/2} = 10\,\text{rad/s}$，$t=0$ 时 $x_0=A=0.05\text{m}$，$v_0=0$

　　振动方程：$x(t) = 0.05\cos(10t)\,\text{m}$

（2）静止时弹簧伸长量 $x_{\text{静}} = mg/k = 20/200 = 0.1\,\text{m}$

　　最低点时总伸长量 $= 0.1 + 0.05 = 0.15\,\text{m}$

　　弹簧拉力 $F = kx = 200 \times 0.15 = 30\,\text{N}$

（3）从最低点 $(x=A)$ 到平衡位置 $(x=0)$：$t = T/4 = \dfrac{2\pi/\omega}{4} = \dfrac{\pi}{20} \approx 0.157\,\text{s}$

---

**3.**
（1）$\omega = 2\pi f = 4\pi$，$\lambda = u/f = 10\,\text{m}$，$k = 2\pi/\lambda = 0.2\pi$

　　波沿 $-x$ 方向传播，$t=0$，$x=0$ 时 $y=0$ 且 $v_y>0$ → $\varphi = -\pi/2$

　　波动方程：$y(x,t) = 0.02\sin(4\pi t + 0.2\pi x)\,\text{m}$

（2）$x' = x - 5$，代入得 $y(x',t) = 0.02\sin(4\pi t + 0.2\pi x' + \pi) = -0.02\sin(4\pi t + 0.2\pi x')\,\text{m}$

---

**4.**
（1）$W = nRT\ln\dfrac{V_2}{V_1} = 0.5 \times 8.31 \times 300 \times \ln 4 = 1246.5 \times 1.386 \approx 1.73 \times 10^3\,\text{J}$

（2）等温过程 $\Delta U = 0$，$Q = W \approx 1.73 \times 10^3\,\text{J}$

---

**5.**
（1）对重物：$mg - T = ma$；对滑轮：$TR = J\alpha = \dfrac{1}{2}MR^2 \cdot \dfrac{a}{R}$，得 $T = \dfrac{1}{2}Ma$

　　联立：$mg - \dfrac{1}{2}Ma = ma$，代入数据 $10 - \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \times a = 1 \times a$

　　得 $10 - a = a$，$a = 5\,\text{m/s}^2$

（2）$T = \dfrac{1}{2}Ma = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 5 = 5\,\text{N}$
